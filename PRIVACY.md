# Privacy Policy for Power

_Last updated: October 21, 2026_

Power is a weekly routine planner. This policy explains what happens to your information
when you use the Power app on Android, iPhone, or the web.

## The short version

**Power does not collect, store, or share any of your personal information.** There are no
accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. Your routine never leaves your device
unless you choose to export it.

## What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter — events, tasks, times, notes, statuses, and settings — is saved only
on your own device, in the app's private storage. The developer has no server and cannot
see, access, or recover this information.

If you uninstall the app or clear its data, your routine is deleted from the device.

## Network access

Power does not send your information over the internet. It contains no third-party
analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting code, and its fonts and images are built into
the app.

## Notifications

If you allow notifications, Power schedules reminders with your device's own notification
system. Reminder details (an item's title, time, and notes) are handed only to your
device's operating system so it can show them at the right time. They are not sent
anywhere else.

## Exporting your data

If you use **File › Export**, Power creates a backup file and opens your device's share
sheet. Where that file goes (for example Files, Google Drive, or email) is your choice and
is covered by that service's own privacy policy.

## Children

Power does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children
under 13.
